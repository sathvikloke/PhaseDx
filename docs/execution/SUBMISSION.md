## Required for the planned externally evaluated study

- The frozen primary external comparison is executed, including full-source flow, missing predictions, reference/grouping and adapted-comparator limits. Scientific reviewers accept or accurately qualify the evidence, regardless of whether the effect is positive.
- Retained MRI/breast/internal CT Methods and target definitions are source-bound; unresolved historical facts are described honestly. A new full training campaign is not automatically required to admit a limitation.
- Existing synthetic findings are framed as constructed examples/planning assumptions, not observed clinical utility or proof of an actual model's causal mechanism.
- All central claims, tables and figures are checked against the actual populations, endpoints, signed contrasts and uncertainty definitions. Exploratory work remains labeled; absence of significance is not equivalence.
- Source selection/exposure chronology is truthful. Existing repository freezes are not backdated prospective registration.
- The final manuscript and supplement answer one coherent question. The old prevalence screen and every historical dataset are not required additions.
- Ethics/secondary-use/consent or waiver basis, release permissions, data/code/model availability, funding, conflicts, actual AI assistance and author contributions are resolved by the responsible humans.
- Final roster, author agreement, current format/checklist and rendered-file checks pass H14. No artificial task-count percentage substitutes for these decisions.

## What Claude may do

Claude may prepare runnable code, execute the preapproved design, retrieve factual primary-source metadata, assemble field-to-evidence matrices, render plots from approved specifications and typeset approved text. It may not independently validate its own scientific conclusions, claim ethics approval, assign authorship, accept unknown terms, send correspondence or submit a manuscript.

H01/H03–H13 reserve design, clinical judgments, verification, interpretation and writing for the user/Codex or an appropriately qualified human. H13 waits for the user's final roster without blocking unrelated implementation. There are no four collaborator compute assignments.

## Narrower route if chosen explicitly

A methodological audit with carefully bounded internal/synthetic claims can be evaluated as a different scope if the external study cannot be completed. This is a scientific decision under H01/H11, not permission for Claude to drop the main empirical milestone and declare completion. Likewise, external data availability does not justify claiming clinical generalization before results and review.

Journal submission, release publication and author contact are distinct actions. Preparing them is included in the queue; actual external action requires its applicable explicit instruction. The present GitHub backlog push does not submit the paper.
