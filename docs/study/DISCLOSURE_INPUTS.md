## Interpretation boundaries

The current manuscript may report recorded source identities, conditional retained estimates, the controlled simulation counterexample and limitations. It cannot yet report external clinical accuracy. Lack of a statistically resolved paired increment is not equivalence; geometry association does not prove the image model's causal mechanism. Current source-history gaps should be disclosed once, not used to trigger repeated unrelated simulations or claim that all useful work is impossible.

The bibliography was generated from 14 retained Crossref primary metadata records with full author lists and matching DOI/title identities. This verifies citation identity only. It does not establish novelty, journal acceptance or the truth of a claim attributed to a paper. The references and source-supported Methods must undergo author review with the final scientific text.
