## Study components and analysis units

The study combines retained MRI analyses, an internal CT comparison, two fixed synthetic experiments, and a planned independent CQ500 examination-level CT evaluation. These components address different questions and use different units. We do not pool their AUROCs or interpret two optimization seeds as two independent patient replications.

The retained prostate data comprise 45 DWI subjects with 1,359 slices and 67 T2 subjects with 2,039 slices. Thirty-eight recorded identifiers overlap between sequences; they are related-sequence analyses. The project target is reader-derived PI-RADS suspicion at or above 3, not established pathology-confirmed cancer. The source publication describes the released annotations; the local threshold and cached rows define the analyzed target. [Tibrewala et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03252-w)

The retained breast analysis uses 140 cached files, 70 local case identifiers and 2,240 slice records, with 54 positive and 16 negative case labels. Case-level lesion status was converted to malignant versus benign/negative and assigned to slices. A slice record is consequently not an independently annotated lesion location. The source release's 300 examinations from 284 participants, the 90 local coded cases on storage, and the 70 evaluated cases are distinct denominators. Repeat-scan linkage and acquisition selection must remain explicit; the locally acquired subset was not a representative random sample. Pathology/follow-up ascertainment for every evaluated case is not established by the inspected records. [Solomon et al.](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.240345)

The retained B15 workbook/index investigation reports three evaluated cases carrying documented repeat flags, with their partner examinations outside the evaluated set and no documented person group containing multiple evaluated cases. Its private mapping and role-overlap files were recovered without recalculating outcomes. This remains a prior producer finding, not a fresh independent workbook audit, and identity beyond the source's repeat flags remains unknown. It supports a limited grouping statement, not proof of absence of all leakage.

The internal CT evaluation comprises 283 studies from 250 recorded patients, with 117 positive and 166 negative study labels and 9,932 slices. The study target was any positive slice in the retained RSNA `stage_2_train` labels. The RSNA source's training annotations must not be described as the triple-reader test reference standard. [Flanders et al.](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.2020190211)

## Source-bound prostate C2 training

The October C2 magnitude analysis consists of 20 completed jobs: two sequences, five original subject folds and two seeds (42 and 123). The canonical 20-job record supersedes an earlier incomplete 19-job summary. C2 is a current-implementation reproduction with retained source/configuration identities; it does not recover missing historical training provenance.

Each job read the existing cached arrays and the original fold-specific training, validation and test membership. The recorded design required existing validation rows to take precedence and prohibited a new validation carve. Magnitude input was standardized per slice. The network was an ImageNet-initialized ResNet-18 with an adapted one-channel stem and a Dropout(0.3)/linear two-class head. All layers were trainable. Training used float32 on CPU, batch size 32, AdamW (learning rate 0.0001, weight decay 0.0001), cross-entropy with label smoothing 0.05, inverse-class-frequency replacement sampling on training rows, two warm-up epochs followed by cosine scheduling, gradient clipping at 1.0, and at most 20 epochs. Early stopping used patience five and minimum improvement 0.0001. Checkpoint selection used validation AUROC, with negative validation loss as the recorded fallback. The selected state was reloaded for the retained prediction identity check.

## Internal CT and frozen external models

The internal pilot assigned ranked patients to fit, comparator/checkpoint selection, head and evaluation partitions. Original geometry fitting used 1,498 of the 1,500 fit patients after its orientation policy. A later fit that included the two orientation-unresolved patients is a sensitivity analysis with later chronology; it does not replace the original primary model or interval table.

The CT image implementation uses a three-channel window representation resized to 224 by 224, the historical float16 storage round trip, float32 ImageNet normalization, and a ResNet-18 logit. The original recipe bytes remain unchanged. The original trainer source records an effective 32-image group with eight-image microbatches, AdamW with learning rate 0.0003 and weight decay 0.0001, one-epoch warm-up then cosine decay, and positive-weighted binary cross-entropy. Actual model epochs and selected checkpoints belong to their run records, not an assumed default for every historical run. Both retained image checkpoints were matched to their original hashes in the CPU session.

The external primary compares seed-42 maximum slice logit with the frozen reconstructed tree using transported physical-rank geometry. This is an explicitly adapted representation, not proof of equivalence to the original slice-counter geometry or original selected G-star. Geometry uses fixed count, span, gap, near-coincidence and 20-bin occupancy features, with training-fitted medians and missingness indicators. No CQ500 labels are used to refit it. Development ranges are support flags, not outcome-informed external exclusions.

The explicit JPEG-Lossless SV1 decoder extension preserves the downstream recipe and must be named and source/package-bound in the pre-outcome design. Twelve fixed compressed-source comparisons agreed exactly between two decoders; this does not establish full-cohort decoder performance or clinical series compatibility. Technical decoding, noncontrast axial soft-kernel brain coverage, and clinical reference validity are separate requirements.

## Retained statistics and planned external statistics

The retained MRI comparison uses within-fold concordant-pair counts, combining numerators and denominators rather than averaging fold AUROCs. Original paired subject-within-fold multiplicities provide conditional percentile intervals. Pooled out-of-fold and within-fold results remain separate. These intervals condition on fitted predictions and fixed folds; they do not include model fitting/selection variability or all dependence from overlapping training sets. Historical score families and C2 score families must never share an unlabeled result row.

The external primary is an examination-weighted paired AUROC difference on the frozen eligible, labeled, jointly scored set. Seed 123 is descriptive on its own available paired set. The fixed clinical recipe uses 2,000 paired bootstrap draws, seed 2026101327 and linear percentiles, retaining undefined draws explicitly. Verified person linkage is preferred; the already specified examination fallback must disclose unresolved repeat-person dependence and cannot be called patient-clustered validation.

Missing-score reporting retains the full intended eligible labeled denominator. Conservative finite-cohort completion bounds address the effect of unobserved scores; they are not sampling confidence intervals and do not eliminate selection bias. The reader target is the fixed three-reader any-ICH majority rule, with two agreeing valid reads required and other configurations missing. Chronic hemorrhage remains positive under that target. The source key transformation and clinical interpretation must be authenticated before a clinical result is accepted.

## Chronology and interpretive limits

The October designs followed earlier public and local outcome inspection. Source timestamps can establish recorded order but do not establish an independently registered, prospectively conceived study. Breast acquisition was label-aware, C2 followed earlier fold results, and the CT eligibility sensitivity followed the original comparison. Describe these analyses as retrospective unless a genuinely earlier registration supports a narrower claim.

The controlled transport simulation supplies a counterexample: an internal image increment over geometry can coexist with external transport failure under its defined generator. It does not show that a real CNN used a particular shortcut. The fixed precision simulation is a hypothetical feasibility calculation, not achieved clinical power. Intervals containing zero do not establish equivalence, and high geometry predictability alone does not identify what a trained image model used.

No CQ500 prediction, external AUROC, clinical superiority or submission readiness is established by this Methods assembly. Remaining factual fields include clinical series qualification, source/person/reference mapping, retained breast ascertainment, cache-production lineage, project ethics/permissions, author contributions and final approvals.
